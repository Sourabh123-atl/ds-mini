🛡️ Surveillance vs Clinical Diagnosis

FLUX measures online discussion volume, hospital strain queries, and public sentiment. It clearly separates social chatter from clinical diagnosis and does not assert medical causation.

🔍 Explainable AI (XAI)

Rather than a black-box model, our supervised TF-IDF + Logistic Regression pipeline provides transparent token attribution weights showing which words drove the sentiment classification.
